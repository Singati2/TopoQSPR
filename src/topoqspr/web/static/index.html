TopoQSPR

Disease-anchored QSPR with verified graph-theoretic topological indices: how much do topological indices predict beyond molecular size?

Exploratory mode. This site runs a fast, reduced analysis. Confirmatory analyses use the command-line protocol.

      3. Choose targets and run

      Computed targets (RDKit)

      Computed targets are atom-contribution sums; they replicate the genre and support no headline claims. MolWt is a sanity check.

      Measured targets (licence-checked sources)

      Values are joined on the full InChIKey of the standardised parent; drugs without a measurement are left out of that target (never imputed). Small cohorts may have too few measured values; such targets are reported as not evaluated.

      4. Progress and results

      Job link (bookmark to come back):

      Exploratory run: one repeat of 5-fold cross-validation, three fast learners, no Y-randomisation or bootstrap. Confidence intervals over five folds are wide. Rows marked underpowered do not support claims (n below the protocol minimum or outer test folds too small).

      Download all results (zip) manifest.csv run_record.json

      ΔR² and ΔMAE are paired differences against the molecular-size baseline (heavy-atom and bond count) on identical folds, with 95 % confidence intervals. AD outside = share of test predictions outside the applicability domain.

      Topological indices for your own molecules

      Paste SMILES (one per line, optionally followed by a name) or a CSV with a smiles column, or choose a file. The result is a zip with every verified index, and the reason for each undefined value.
