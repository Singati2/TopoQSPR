# Related Papers

## Required Reading

These papers are required background for anyone contributing to TopoQSPR.

### 1. Arockiaraj, Greeni & Abul Kalaam (2023)

**Title:** *Linear versus cubic regression models for analyzing generalized reverse degree based topological indices of certain latest corona treatment drug molecules*

**Authors:** Micheal Arockiaraj, A. Berin Greeni, AR. Abul Kalaam

**Journal:** *International Journal of Quantum Chemistry* 123 (2023), e27136

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1002/qua.27136

**Access:** Paywalled (© 2023 Wiley Periodicals LLC). Read it through the DOI with institutional access. The PDF is deliberately not stored in this public repository.

#### Relevance to TopoQSPR

This paper is directly related to TopoQSPR because it develops generalized/modified reverse-degree topological descriptors for molecular graphs and evaluates them using QSPR analysis. It compares linear and cubic regression models for predicting physicochemical properties of drug molecules from graph-theoretic topological indices. This makes it a useful methodological and literature reference for TopoQSPR's broader automated molecular-descriptor and predictive-modeling framework.

### 2. Arockiaraj, Paul, Clement, Tigga, Jacob & Balasubramanian (2023)

**Title:** *Novel molecular hybrid geometric-harmonic-Zagreb degree based descriptors and their efficacy in QSPR studies of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons*

**Authors:** M. Arockiaraj, D. Paul, J. Clement, S. Tigga, K. Jacob, K. Balasubramanian

**Journal:** *SAR and QSAR in Environmental Research* 34(7) (2023), 569–589

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1080/1062936X.2023.2239149

**PDF (partial):** [`Arockiaraj_2023_SARQSAR_hybrid_GHZ_descriptors_photos.pdf`](Arockiaraj_2023_SARQSAR_hybrid_GHZ_descriptors_photos.pdf). These are 29 phone photos of the article on screen, cropped to the article pages and covering pp. 570–585: the introduction, the bi-/tri-Zagreb (BM, TM) and ten hybrid descriptor definitions (GH, GBM, GTM, HG, HBM, HTM, BMG, BMH, TMG, TMH), Figures 1–2 (the 55 benzenoid hydrocarbons B1–B55), the M-polynomial derivation, Table 1 (bond partitions), Table 2 (descriptor values), Table 3 (experimental log P, RI, BP, ω), the regression equations with Figure 3 and Table 4 (correlations), Figure 4 and Table 5 (predictions for 10 external PAHs), the circumcoronene series H_n (Figure 5, Table 6) and the Conclusion. Pages overlap, some are slightly blurry, and the landscape Table 2 pages are rotated upright. **Not included:** the title page and abstract (p. 569), and the disclosure statement and references (pp. 585–589). Get the full text through the DOI.

#### Relevance to TopoQSPR

This paper introduces ten hybrid degree-based descriptors built from ratios and products of the geometric, harmonic, bi-Zagreb and tri-Zagreb indices. It computes them through M-polynomials with differential/integral operators, and tests them with linear QSPR regressions on physicochemical properties of 55 benzenoid hydrocarbons (PAHs). Every compound in the set has only degree-2/3 vertices, so each descriptor is a fixed function of the three edge-partition counts (d2,2, d2,3, d3,3). That makes the paper a direct benchmark for TopoQSPR's descriptor-generation and QSPR-evaluation pipeline, and the 55-PAH set is a standard reference dataset.
