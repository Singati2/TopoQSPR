**What is stored here.** Under CC BY 4.0 this folder stores small factual tables taken from the article: the drug names as printed, the per-drug property values (`properties.csv`), the index formulas and the reported fit statistics (`study.json`). Changes: values are reshaped to long format, and each printed string is kept next to its parsed number. Formulas are rendered from MathML/TeX into linear text. No prose, figures or images are copied.
